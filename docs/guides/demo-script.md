# Demo script (short beat sheet)

Full timed presentation: [`demo-walkthrough.md`](demo-walkthrough.md) ([PDF](CueClose-Demo-Walkthrough.pdf)).

## Narrative

CueClose closes a live arena tour's books while the trucks are already rolling to the next city. Ambient cues arrive with source evidence. The system recommends. Humans decide. The ledger stays exact.

## Beats

1. **Splash**  
   Brand moment. Tagline: the tour keeps moving; the books keep up.

2. **Command (Classic)**  
   Horizon Arena Tour metrics, show slate, policy version, actor switcher.

3. **Material approval**  
   Open `cue_001` (Northwind Rigging, $8,650). Show evidence, confidence, policy notes. Approve as Maya (tour controller). Post. Show balanced journal.

4. **Authority denial**  
   Switch to Jordan (production manager). Attempt to approve another material cue. Surface the policy code.

5. **Hold path**  
   Open Clearwave RF (`cue_004`). Show low-confidence hold reason.

6. **Reconciliation + Audit**  
   Explain a variance without changing ledger or legacy amounts. Scroll seed, review, approval, post, explain.

7. **Pro edition**  
   Toggle Edition to Pro. Waiting-on-me queue, show filters, duplicate acknowledge gate on the near-duplicate Stagecraft cue.

## Guardrails to say out loud

- Models recommend; they do not post.
- Money is exact decimal, never binary float.
- Channels in this demo are simulated.
- All tour data is synthetic.
